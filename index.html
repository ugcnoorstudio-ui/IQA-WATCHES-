03 · Watch size guide

Understand the fit.

Measure your wrist with a flexible tape, or wrap a paper strip around your wrist and measure it against a ruler.

Case diameter, thickness, lug-to-lug length and strap width are different measurements. Product-specific sizing recommendations will appear only when verified dimensions are available.

Fit is approximate and also depends on lug-to-lug length, case shape, wrist shape, strap design and personal preference. No fit is guaranteed.
